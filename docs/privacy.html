PRIVACY POLICY

TRICE  ·  last updated 9 September 2026

The short version

Trice does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking built into the game.

What stays on your device

Your scores, statistics, achievements, unlocked skins and themes, daily streak, and settings (sound, last mode) are saved locally on your device using standard browser/app storage. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you delete the app or clear its data.

Network

The game runs fully offline. It does not make network requests of its own.

Advertising

The version of Trice distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs. If Trice is played on a third-party web game portal (for example Poki or CrazyGames), that portal — not Trice — may serve ads and apply its own privacy policy to your visit. Trice does not receive personal data from those portals.

Children

Trice is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
